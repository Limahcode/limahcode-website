FAQ

Questions from parents

What does my child need?
A laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac, or Linux), stable internet, and Zoom installed. We'll help set up any other free tools before the first class.
When are classes held?
Weekend sessions — Saturday mornings and/or Sunday afternoons, Lagos time (WAT). Exact schedule shared upon confirmation. Sessions are 90 minutes each.
Is there homework?
Yes — small, fun challenges each week taking 30–60 minutes. Our Discord is available for help between sessions.
What if my child misses a class?
All sessions are recorded and shared within 24 hours. Missing one class won't derail progress, but consistent attendance gives the best outcome.
How much does it cost?
Beginner: ₦25,000 · Builder: ₦35,000 · Advanced: ₦45,000 per 8-week cohort. Sibling discount of ₦5,000 off per additional student. Payment via Paystack.
What does my child get at the end?
A LimahCode certificate of completion, a live deployed project, a portfolio entry, and access to our alumni community. Advanced graduates also get a letter of recommendation.

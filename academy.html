FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the tuition and what does it cover?
Tuition is ₦50,000 per student for the entire 3-month (12-week) program. It covers live classes, interactive coding portal access, weekly mentorship, project reviews, and your graduation certificate.
What equipment do I or my child need?
A laptop or desktop computer (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook) with stable internet access and Zoom/Google Meet installed. No prior coding software is required — we will set up everything together!
When are classes held?
• Teens Track (9–17): Weekend live sessions on Saturdays and Sundays.
• Adult Track (18+): Weekday evening sessions designed to accommodate working professionals and university students.
Can a complete beginner with zero experience join?
Yes! Cohort 2 is designed specifically for complete beginners. We start from ground zero (how the internet and browsers work) and build up step-by-step.
What if I miss a live class?
Every live session is recorded and uploaded to the student area within 24 hours so you can rewatch lessons anytime.
Will students master JavaScript in 1 month?
In Month 3, students master Frontend JavaScript DOM Interactivity — how to make buttons work, toggle dark mode, build click counters, interactive forms, and mini web games. This gives practical, rewarding website-building skills without being overwhelmed by abstract backend algorithms. Deep React and backend frameworks continue in our advanced masterclass!
